# Gate D2 — Definition & Ideation Review

## Purpose
Human approval of UX definition, candidate directions, and concept selection before design begins.

## Artefacts reviewed in order
1. outputs/phase-02/problem-definition.md
2. outputs/phase-02/journey-map.md
3. outputs/phase-02/opportunities.md
4. outputs/phase-02/concept-options.md
5. outputs/phase-02/selected-concept.md

## Review checklist
- Problem statements are supported by approved Phase 1 evidence and/or clearly identified business constraints.
- User evidence and business requirements remain distinguishable.
- Journey stages are evidence-based or marked hypothetical.
- Opportunities are hypotheses, not committed features.
- Multiple solution directions were considered when evidence supported them.
- Concepts distinguish evidence-supported problems, design hypotheses, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
- The selected concept documents alternatives, common criteria, evidence, risks, and rationale.
- No unsupported requirement was introduced during ideation.
- Priority rationale is transparent.
- Assumptions, contradictions, dependencies, and validation needs are visible.

## Human decisions
- APPROVE — clear D2 and allow Phase 3.
- REVISE — provide actionable feedback and rerun only affected skill plus downstream dependencies.
- REJECT — stop at Phase 2.

## Dependency logic
- If problem-definition.md changes, reassess journey-map.md and opportunities.md.
- If only journey-map.md changes, reassess opportunities.md when the change affects prioritization.
- If opportunities.md changes materially, regenerate concept-options.md and selected-concept.md.
- If only concept-options.md changes, rerun concept evaluation.

## Governance
- Agents cannot approve D2.
- Only explicit human APPROVE advances the workflow.
- Record a decision in outputs/reviews/d2-review.md and update project.state.md.
