# Gate D4 — Final UX Validation Review

## Purpose
Human review of validation evidence before the prototype may be called verified/workable.

## Artefacts reviewed in order
1. outputs/phase-04/validation-report.md
2. outputs/phase-04/validation-issues.md
3. outputs/phase-04/requirement-coverage.md
4. outputs/phase-04/accessibility-audit.md
5. D3-approved prototype artefacts

## Review checklist
- Synthetic usability and cognitive-friction findings disclose their heuristic limitations.
- Accessibility conclusions do not overclaim WCAG compliance.
- Design-system evidence is audited or marked NOT AVAILABLE.
- Relevant requirements have explicit coverage statuses.
- Phase 1 risks and important failure states have current dispositions.
- Unresolved critical issues and human decisions are visible.

## Human decisions
- APPROVE — mark the prototype verified/workable and complete the documented workflow.
- REVISE — route to the minimum affected Phase 3 skill/output, regenerate dependent prototype work, rerun relevant Phase 4 checks, and return to D4.
- REJECT — stop and record the reason.

## Governance
- Agents and automated checks cannot approve D4.
- Silence or a generic request to continue is not approval.
- Record the decision in outputs/reviews/d4-review.md and update project.state.md.
