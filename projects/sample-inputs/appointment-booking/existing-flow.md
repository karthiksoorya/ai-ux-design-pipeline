# Existing Flow

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. This describes a fictional current-state phone process.

1. Patient finds a clinic phone number.
2. Patient waits in a call queue.
3. Receptionist asks for service, preferred time, name, date of birth, phone, and reason for visit.
4. Receptionist checks a separate scheduling system.
5. If the slot is unavailable, the receptionist verbally proposes alternatives.
6. Receptionist records the booking and reads back details.
7. The clinic sends an SMS when its notification service is available.

## Known operational constraints

- Reception operates 09:00–17:00 weekdays.
- Staff sometimes place callers on hold while checking availability.
- Cancellation policy differs by service and is not documented in this pack.
