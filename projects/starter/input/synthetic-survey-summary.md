# Synthetic Survey Summary

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. No real survey was conducted.

## Simulated sample

- 60 fictional respondents who had attempted to book a routine appointment in the previous year.
- Convenience sample; not representative of any clinic population.

## Simulated results

- 42/60 preferred seeing available times before entering personal information.
- 31/60 had booked for another person at least once.
- 27/60 preferred SMS confirmation, 19 preferred email, and 14 wanted both.
- 18/60 reported that unclear error messages would make them call the clinic.
- 12/60 said they sometimes use shared devices.

## Limitations

- Values are authored for pipeline demonstration and are not empirical measurements.
- Questions, recruitment, demographic distribution, and statistical uncertainty are not validated.
- Results may support demo synthesis only when retained with this limitation.
