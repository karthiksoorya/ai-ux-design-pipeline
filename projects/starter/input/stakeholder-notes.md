# Stakeholder Notes

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. These are fictional stakeholder opinions, not user research.

## Product owner

- Wants a demonstrable booking flow within eight weeks.
- Believes requiring both email and phone will improve successful contact.
- Wants marketing consent visible but has not supplied legal approval for default opt-in.

## Reception lead

- Wants booking references visible during support calls.
- Says slot conflicts are common in the current phone process.
- Has not defined who resolves delayed scheduling-service responses.

## Privacy reviewer

- Requests data minimization and a retention decision for incomplete forms.
- Flags caregiver booking and confirmation recipients for human/legal review.

## Engineering representative

- Confirms the scheduling service can return success, rejection, timeout, or unavailable responses.
- Cannot guarantee email and SMS delivery status in the first release.
