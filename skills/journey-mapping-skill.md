# Journey Mapping Skill

## Name & Description
Creates a traceable current-state or target-hypothesis journey from approved personas, pain points, and problem definition.

## Role
Support the UX Definition Agent in exposing stages, goals, actions, touchpoints, emotions/frictions, and evidence gaps.

## Input
Approved Phase 1 outputs and outputs/phase-02/problem-definition.md.

## Output
outputs/phase-02/journey-map.md

## Rules & Guardrails
- Mark hypothetical stages or behaviors as LOW confidence.
- Do not invent user behavior to make the journey look complete.
- Preserve evidence citations and gaps.
