# Opportunity Prioritization Skill

## Name & Description
Identifies and ranks UX opportunity hypotheses based on supported pain points, journey breakdowns, user value, business relevance, feasibility signals, dependencies, and uncertainty.

## Role
Support the UX Definition Agent in deciding what design space deserves attention first.

## Input
problem-definition.md, journey-map.md, pain-points.md, and relevant business constraints.

## Output
outputs/phase-02/opportunities.md

## Rules & Guardrails
- Opportunities are not confirmed requirements.
- Explain ranking logic.
- Low-evidence opportunities must remain low confidence.
