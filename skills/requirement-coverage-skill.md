# Requirement Coverage Skill

## Name & Description
Traces relevant BRD requirements through approved definition, concept, design, and prototype artefacts.

## Role
Support the UX Validation & Audit Agent in exposing incomplete or undecidable coverage.

## Input
BRD/source inventory and approved Phase 1–3 artefacts.

## Output
outputs/phase-04/requirement-coverage.md

## Rules & Guardrails
- Classify each relevant requirement as COVERED, PARTIALLY COVERED, NOT COVERED, NOT APPLICABLE, or REQUIRES HUMAN DECISION.
- Include source locator and design/prototype locator.
- Do not convert assumptions or risk hypotheses into requirements.
